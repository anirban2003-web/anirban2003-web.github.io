01 — ABOUT

Building towards astrophysics and cosmology.

I am a B.Sc. (Hons.) Physics student at Ramjas College, University of Delhi, with a growing focus on astrophysics, cosmology, and computational physics.

My current work focuses on extracting cosmological information from astronomical observations and combining independent probes to understand the expansion history of the Universe.

I am particularly interested in the intersection of observational data, Bayesian inference, and fundamental physics.

In the longer term, I hope to pursue research in astrophysics and cosmology, with interests extending toward gravitation and fundamental physics.
