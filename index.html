01 — ABOUT

Building towards astrophysics and cosmology.

I am a B.Sc. (Hons.) Physics student at Ramjas College, University of Delhi, with a growing focus on astrophysics and cosmology.

My current work is centered on observational cosmology, particularly the use of independent astronomical probes to study the expansion history of the Universe.

I am interested in combining astronomical observations with statistical and computational methods to investigate cosmological parameters and tensions within the standard cosmological framework.

Alongside observational cosmology, I am interested in gravitation, quantum mechanics, and fundamental questions concerning the physics of the Universe.

Research projects

OBSERVATIONAL COSMOLOGY

The Hubble Tension

A literature-based investigation of the discrepancy between early- and late-Universe measurements of the Hubble constant, examining the cosmic distance ladder, CMB constraints, Type Ia supernovae, BAO and proposed extensions to ΛCDM.

Cosmology H₀ ΛCDM DESI SNe Ia
02

COSMOLOGICAL PARAMETER INFERENCE

Multi-Probe Cosmological Inference

Developing a joint statistical framework for combining independent cosmological probes, including supernovae, cosmic chronometers, DES observations and quasar data.

MCMC Bayesian Inference SNe CC QSO
03

AGN COSMOLOGY

AGN as Distance Indicators

Investigating empirical relations involving active galactic nuclei and quasars as secondary distance indicators, with the broader goal of assessing their potential for cosmological applications.

AGN Quasars Distance Indicators Cosmology
04

COMPUTATIONAL COSMOLOGY

Gaussian Processes in Cosmology

Exploring Gaussian-process methods for reconstructing astronomical observables and estimating uncertainties without imposing a specific parametric form on the underlying function.

Gaussian Processes Non-parametric Methods Astrophysics
