SELECTED WORK

Research, analysis and computational projects.

A selection of my current and completed work in cosmology, astrophysics and computational physics.

01

LITERATURE REVIEW · COSMOLOGY

The Hubble Tension

A literature review investigating the discrepancy between early- and late-Universe measurements of the Hubble constant.

The project examines the cosmic distance ladder, CMB-based inference, Type Ia supernovae, BAO, calibration methods and proposed modifications to the standard ΛCDM framework.

Particular attention is given to the role of different datasets and assumptions in determining the inferred value of H₀.

Hubble Tension ΛCDM CMB SNe Ia BAO DESI
02

DISSERTATION · COSMOLOGICAL INFERENCE

Multi-Probe Cosmology with AGN

My current dissertation work investigates the use of active galactic nuclei and quasars as secondary distance indicators for cosmological studies.

The analysis combines multiple astronomical probes, including supernovae, cosmic chronometers, DES observations and quasar data, within a joint likelihood framework.

The broader objective is to investigate how independent distance and expansion indicators can be combined to constrain cosmological models and nuisance parameters.

AGN Quasars Supernovae Cosmic Chronometers MCMC Bayesian Inference
03

COMPUTATIONAL ASTROPHYSICS

Gaussian-Process Reconstruction

An exploration of Gaussian-process methods for reconstructing cosmological observables directly from observational data.

The project investigates how non-parametric statistical methods can reconstruct smooth functions and propagate observational uncertainties without assuming a specific parametric model.

Gaussian Processes GaPP Non-parametric Inference Cosmology
04

NUMERICAL PHYSICS

Computational Methods in Quantum Physics

Numerical studies of quantum-mechanical systems using computational methods such as finite-difference discretisation, matrix methods and numerical solutions of the Schrödinger equation.

These projects have helped develop my broader interest in numerical modelling and computational approaches to physics.

Quantum Mechanics Numerical Methods Python Schrödinger Equation
